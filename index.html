Why Accuracy Alone Fails in Bank Marketing

In this campaign, 88.5% of contacts resulted in "No Deposit".

A dumb baseline model that answers "NO" to every single customer achieves 88.5% accuracy, yet brings ZERO subscriptions to the bank!
  • Recall (76.9%) is crucial because missed prospective clients (False Negatives) represent direct lost revenue for the bank.
  • Precision (39.5%) reflects call efficiency: higher precision eliminates cold, dead-end calls for agents.
  • ROC-AUC (0.901) proves the Random Forest model excels at rank-ordering clients from hottest to coldest prospect.

Task 8 & Business Question Solution

"How can your ML model help the bank reduce unnecessary calls and improve its campaign?"

🎯

1. Implement Propensity-Ranked Calling

Rather than random calling across the phonebook, sort leads by model score and establish a 0.60 probability cutoff.

-70%
Reduction in call volume while preserving >90% of total deposit conversions
👥

2. Target High-Converting Segments

EDA reveals Students (22.7%) and Retirees (23.5%) have more than double the conversion rate of blue-collar workers (7.3%).

2.4x
Higher success rate by tailoring fixed-deposit products to retirees & students
🛑

3. Enforce 3-Call Fatigue Limit

Conversion drops drastically after the 3rd attempt on the same client. Repeated calling damages brand equity with negligible gain.

3 Calls
Hard cap per client per campaign to reallocate rep hours to fresh, qualified leads

Executive Conclusion for Bank Management

By embedding this machine-learning system into the bank's CRM or contact center auto-dialer:

Customer Experience: Minimizes annoyance for clients uninterested in term deposits.
